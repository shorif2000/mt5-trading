## Status and assumptions

- Paper ledger: starts at £1000 for each period; 2% risk per trade.
- Cost scenario: entry stop-median-proxy (7 pts, SCENARIO) / stop exits p90 (40 pts, evidence) / sizing allowance 67 pts.
- Commission: £0 round turn per lot, verified (Standard swap-free account; 18,466 historical XAUUSD deals all with commission 0).
- Margin: MT5 calc_margin over one read-only session.
- **Not news-filtered:** no economic calendar source (Version 1.0 decision).
- **Real-yield filter (Version 1.1):** FRED DFII10 change over 20 published observations; <= -0.10 pp longs only, >= +0.10 pp shorts only, otherwise both. An observation is used from 17:00 New York on the next business day after its date. FRED's current vintage only (revisions cannot be checked).
- **Out of sample is not blind:** the 2026 period was already viewed in the Version 1.0 reports before Version 1.1 was specified.
- Fills: longs buy at the ask (entry bar spread paid) and sell at the bid; shorts sell at the bid and buy back at the ask (exit bar spread paid). Stop exits slip by the evidenced stop percentile; take profits fill at the target; entries and market exits (forced close, trend exit) slip by the entry scenario. Stop assumed first when both are touched in one bar; gaps fill at the open. One spread value per bar, so intrabar spikes are not modelled.
- Target: 2R from the quoted entry (Appendix A.F); the planned reward:risk at the actual fill is reported separately and is below 2 after entry slippage.
- Forced close 16:45 New York; no entries 16:00-19:00 New York; no overnight positions.
- Margin: MT5 `calc_margin` at the historical entry price, but with today's leverage and today's GBPUSD for the margin conversion.
- Loss tick values use GBPUSD at each historical entry (MT5 provides no historical tick values).
