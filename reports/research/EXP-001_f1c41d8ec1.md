## Pre-registered decision rule

- **BE-0.5**: gross R/trade above the control in all three periods (base): no; also in the 7- and 40-point entry scenarios: no → does not pass the historical screen.
- **BE-0.75**: gross R/trade above the control in all three periods (base): no; also in the 7- and 40-point entry scenarios: no → does not pass the historical screen.
- **BE-1.0**: gross R/trade above the control in all three periods (base): no; also in the 7- and 40-point entry scenarios: no → does not pass the historical screen.
- **BE-1.0+C**: gross R/trade above the control in all three periods (base): no; also in the 7- and 40-point entry scenarios: no → does not pass the historical screen.

No variant is selected from these periods. Version 1.0 has no gross edge (diagnostic review), so no break-even rule is adopted whatever the screen says.

## Limitations

- Fixed entry list: trades Version 1.0 skipped while a position was open stay skipped even when a break-even exit would have freed the slot.
- Non-base scenarios replay the base list without re-sizing; only the base control reconciles with a Version 1.0 run.
- One spread value per M15 bar; stop first when a bar touches stop and target.
- Ticks exist only from 2025-06-13; Training cannot be tick-resolved.
- The trigger bar's own exit uses Version 1.0's stop-first convention.
- Not news-filtered (Version 1.0 has no calendar).
